oliver stares at the key turning it over in his hands. it feels cold heavier than it should be. his first thought isnt caution or fear its excitement. maybe theres something valuable in there maybe he can sell old toys online or get content for his stupid channel. his selfish brain takes over and he forgets about his half eaten breakfast completely.

he shoves the key in his pocket and runs back inside to grab his hoodie. he doesnt text his mom doesnt leave a note doesnt think about her at all. shes just at work anyway she wont know. he grabs his phone sees the battery is at 67% good enough and heads out the door slamming it just like his mom did but for a different reason.

oliver hesitates for one second. just one. then he thinks about what could be inside and he ducks through the gap.

inside its dark and it smells like rust and old dust and something sweet underneath like a candy that rotted a long time ago. half built toys sit on broken conveyor belts. doll faces with no eyes stare at him from assembly lines. there are boxes everywhere some crushed some still sealed with logos he doesnt recognize. the floor is covered in glass and papers and bird droppings.

his phone buzzes. he pulls it out and sees the battery is now at 43%. thats weird. he just had it at 67%. but he shakes it off and keeps walking deeper in.

then he hears it. a music box. soft and slow and playing somewhere in the dark ahead of him.

oliver stops. his selfish brain says keep going its probably nothing. but his feet feel heavy now. he takes another step and the music stops. then his phone rings.

its the unknown number again.

he answers and the voice is different now. its shaking whispering like someone is right next to the person with a gun to their head.

"you shouldnt have come inside. it knows youre here now. it collects selfish kids. dont take anything. dont touch anything. and whatever you do dont look behind the"

the call cuts out. static. then the music box starts again but this time its coming from right behind him.

oliver turns around slow and sees a figure standing between two conveyor belts. its small like a child but it moves wrong like its joints are bending backwards. it holds a music box in one hand and in the other hand it holds a phone. a cracked phone with a screen that still glows.

the figure tilts its head and oliver hears his own voice coming from its mouth.

"check your mailbox."

oliver stumbles backwards and trips over a box. he scrambles to his feet and runs. he doesnt know which way is out he just runs past the doll faces and the broken toys and the conveyor belts that start creaking like theyre turning back on. behind him he hears footsteps but theyre not running theyre walking slow like they know he cant escape.

he finds a door and throws it open and slams it shut behind him. its dark in here darker than before. he pulls out his phone to use the light and the battery is at 2%. the screen flickers and shows a photo he never took. its him standing in this exact room but in the photo hes not alone. theres something behind him with its hands on his shoulders and its face is blurred out.

the phone dies. the screen goes black.

and in the darkness oliver hears breathing. not his own. its coming from every direction all at once.

a voice whispers right next to his ear. not the callers voice. something older. something that sounds like it remembers when the toys were alive and the factory was full of children who never came home.

"you didnt think twice," it says. "so now you get to stay and play forever."

oliver wants to scream but his mouth wont open. he wants to run but his legs wont move. he feels cold hands grab his wrists and start pulling him deeper into the dark.

and somewhere far away his mom is coming home from work to an empty house with a cold breakfast still on the table and a front door left wide open.

# THE ABANDONED TOY FACTORY
## Game Design Document (Intro Sequence: House → Factory)

**Game Title:** The Abandoned Toy Factory  
**Platform:** Roblox Studio  
**Genre:** First-Person Narrative Horror / Mystery  
**Target Audience:** Ages 13+  
**Duration (Act 1-3):** 2-3 minutes  
**Camera:** First-Person Perspective  

---

## 1. GAME OVERVIEW

### Core Premise
The player controls Oliver, a selfish and lazy teenager who receives an anonymous phone call directing him to an abandoned toy factory. The message contains cryptic instructions and a warning. Ignoring all caution—driven by greed and selfishness—Oliver ventures to the factory. This intro sequence establishes Oliver's character, the mystery's genesis, and the creeping dread that builds as he approaches his destination.

### Theme
**"Think twice before doing something."**
- Oliver's selfish nature causes him to act without consideration
- Every choice he makes is reactive, not thoughtful
- The consequences of his haste will unfold

### Tone
- **Opening (House):** Mundane, lazy, relatable
- **The Call & Discovery:** Intrigue mixed with unease
- **The Walk:** Growing dread, isolation, atmospheric tension
- **Factory Approach:** Claustrophobic dread, visual horror

---

## 2. SCENE BREAKDOWN

### ACT 1: THE HOUSE (0:00 – 1:00)

#### Scene 1A: The Bedroom (0:00 – 0:15)
**Objective:** Establish Oliver's character and lazy personality. Set the mundane tone.

**Setting:**
- Modern bedroom, dark lighting (early morning)
- Phone on desk or in Oliver's hand
- Clothes on floor, posters on wall
- Small window showing grey/early morning light

**Sequence:**
1. **Fade In** — Camera opens looking at phone screen (scrolling animation shows TikTok/Instagram)
2. **Ambient Sounds** — Soft background noise: birds chirping faintly outside, house settling
3. **Duration:** Camera stays on phone for ~3 seconds of idle scrolling
4. **Player Action:** Player can look around the room (first-person control)
5. **Voice Line (Mom, off-screen):** "OLIVER! I'm going to work, I set up breakfast for you!"
6. **Camera Transition:** Mom's footsteps trigger player to lower phone and look toward door

**Audio:**
- Ambient bedroom sound
- Mom's voice (warm but exasperated)
- Footsteps approaching from hallway

**Technical Notes:**
- Use TweenService for smooth phone lowering animation
- Pre-record mom's voice line (female voice, tired but loving tone)
- Subtle background music: minimalist, low piano

---

#### Scene 1B: Kitchen & Breakfast (0:15 – 0:40)
**Objective:** Show the relationship between Oliver and his mom. Establish that Oliver ignores her concerns.

**Setting:**
- Kitchen is warm and clean
- Mom in winter coat/hat
- Breakfast on table (cereal, toast, juice)
- Door to outside visible

**Sequence:**
1. **Mom's Departure Cutscene (0:15 – 0:25)**
   - Mom walks from hallway to door
   - She sighs (audible frustration)
   - She puts on coat and hat
   - Dialogue: "Oliver, please turn off your phone and eat."
   - She reaches door, pauses (looking back at Oliver)
   - Dialogue: "I'll be back at 6. Please... just be responsible."
   - **SLAM** — Door closes loudly (sound echoes)
   - This door slam is important: it's a beat that shows her frustration

2. **Player Control Resumes (0:25 – 0:35)**
   - Player can now move freely
   - Player walks to kitchen, sits at table
   - Camera focuses on breakfast (food is still warm/steaming)
   - Player sits (camera lowers to sitting eye-level)
   - Food appears on table in front of player
   - Ambient kitchen sounds: clock ticking, fridge humming

3. **Phone Call Interrupts (0:35 – 0:40)**
   - Phone buzzes/rings (loud, sudden)
   - Sound: Unknown number calling tone (tense, staccato)
   - **Script Prompt:** "Unknown Number Calling... Accept? (E to Answer)"
   - Player must press E to accept call

**Dialogue & Voice Acting:**
- Mom: "Oliver, please turn off your phone and eat."
- Mom: "I'll be back at 6. Please... just be responsible." (sad, hopeful)
- Phone: Default ringtone, slightly distorted (hint at wrongness)

**Audio Design:**
- Door slam is CRITICAL sound moment (echoes, emphasizes her departure)
- Kitchen ambience: clock, fridge, subtle hum
- Phone ring: loud and jarring (breaks the peace)

**Camera Work:**
- Wide shot of kitchen as mom is in frame
- Camera follows mom to door (player is seated, watching)
- Camera returns to seated position at table
- Phone appears in hand or on table (depending on implementation)

**Technical Notes:**
- Mom is NPC with path animation to door
- Player can't move during cutscene (lock input)
- Phone ring sound should be attention-grabbing
- Use DialogueGUI or custom prompt system for "Accept Call" prompt

---

### ACT 2: THE DISCOVERY (0:40 – 1:40)

#### Scene 2A: The Phone Call (0:40 – 0:50)
**Objective:** Introduce mystery and dread. The call is the catalyst for Oliver's adventure.

**Setting:**
- Still in kitchen, but phone is now in first-person view
- Room feels slightly darker now (psychological shift)

**Sequence:**
1. **Player Accepts Call** (0:40)
   - Phone connects
   - Brief silence (2 seconds of dead air)
   - Static crackle (faint, ominous)

2. **Unknown Voice Message** (0:40 – 0:45)
   - Voice speaks: **"Check your mailbox."**
   - Voice is neutral, slightly robotic
   - Background sounds: faint advertisement jingles (distorted, glitchy)
   - More static after the message
   - Call ends abruptly (no goodbye, just dial tone)

3. **Player Reacts** (0:45 – 0:50)
   - Oliver's inner monologue (selfish thoughts):
     - *"Huh? Mailbox? What's this about?"*
     - *"Could be something valuable... maybe I can sell it online?"*
   - Camera tilts down (Oliver looking at phone in confusion)
   - Phone screen shows "Unknown Number" in call log
   - Player regains movement control

**Dialogue (Voice Acting):**
- Unknown Caller: **"Check your mailbox."** (male or gender-neutral, calm and eerie)

**Audio Design:**
- Silence (unsettling)
- Static crackle (glitchy, digital distortion)
- Distorted advertisement sound in background (about "new toy released")
- Dial tone at end (abrupt cut-off)
- Oliver's inner monologue (separate audio track, internal voice, slightly greedy tone)

**Technical Notes:**
- Use PhoneGui to display call interface
- Pre-record voiceover for Oliver's inner monologue
- Static SFX: Use audio effect or pre-recorded static
- Call ends automatically after ~5 seconds

**Props & Items:**

**Technical Notes:**
- Create photo as decal/image on part

### ACT 3: THE WALK TO THE FACTORY (1:40 – 2:50)

#### Scene 3A: The Neighborhood (1:40 – 2:10)
**Objective:** Build atmosphere. Show the world getting darker, quieter, emptier. Foreshadow dread.

**Setting:**
- Suburban neighborhood transitioning to industrial area
- Houses get older and more abandoned
- Streets become emptier
- Sky darkens (gray clouds, overcast)

**Sequence:**
1. **Initial Neighborhood (1:40 – 1:55)**
   - Player walks down familiar street
   - Ambient: birds, light breeze, distant traffic
   - Houses are normal, well-maintained
   - A few cars parked on street
   - Oliver's monologue: *"Okay, the factory's on the edge of town. About 45 minutes walk."*

2. **Transition Zone (1:55 – 2:10)**
   - As player walks, neighborhood subtly changes
   - Houses become older, more rundown
   - Some boarded windows, overgrown yards
   - Fewer people visible (no NPCs walking around)
   - Ambient sounds decrease: birds fade, traffic becomes distant
   - Sky gets darker, more overcast
   - Wind picks up slightly
   - Temperature seems to drop (visual cue: Oliver's breath visible in air?)

3. **The Empty Lots (2:10 – 2:25)**
   - Neighborhood ends, houses disappear
   - Now passing empty lots with cracked concrete
   - Abandoned buildings in distance
   - Ambient: wind howling, distant creaking, absolute silence (unnerving)
   - Oliver's monologue becomes less excited: *"This place is... really quiet."*
   - Sky is now dark gray, oppressive
   - No visible sun

**Dialogue (Inner Monologue):**
- Oliver: *"Okay, the factory's on the edge of town. About 45 minutes walk."*
- Oliver: *"This place is... really quiet."*
- Oliver (hesitantly): *"Maybe I should... no. I'm already here. Just a few more minutes."*

**Audio Design:**
- Ambient transitions smoothly:
  - Start: Birds, distant traffic, normal suburb sounds
  - Middle: Fewer birds, less traffic, more wind
  - End: Wind howling, creaking metal (distant), absolute quiet
- Music: Creepy ambient track (low strings, eerie synths)
- Oliver's monologue becomes more unsure (performance shift)

**Visual Design:**
- Sky gradually darkens from bright to overcast
- Grass becomes more overgrown
- Buildings deteriorate in condition
- Camera slightly tilts down (Oliver looking at ground more)
- Fog/mist slightly visible on horizon

**Technical Notes:**
- Create long road/path map piece
- Use terrain or parts to create buildings
- Gradient lighting to darken sky over time
- Ambient sound crossfade using TweenService
- Particle effects: slight fog/mist in distance

## 8. SCRIPTING NOTES & TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

### Scripts Needed

1. **CameraController.lua**
   - First-person camera with mouse/joystick freelook
   - Sensitivity adjustable in settings
   - Head bob animation while walking (subtle)
   - Camera position updates with movement

2. **InputHandler.lua**
   - Detects E key presses for interactions
   - Detects movement (WASD)
   - Detects sprint (Shift)
   - Prevents input during cutscenes

3. **CutsceneManager.lua**
   - Controls cutscene triggers
   - Locks player input during cutscenes
   - Manages camera animations (TweenService)
   - Plays dialogue and sound effects
   - Handles fades and transitions

4. **DialogueSystem.lua**
   - Displays dialogue prompts
   - Triggers voice line playback
   - Inner monologue display (text on screen, fades in/out)
   - Subtitle support

5. **PhoneSystem.lua**
   - Phone call interface GUI
   - Battery drain system (real-time percentage decrease)
   - Unknown number caller simulation
   - Phone screen effects (glitch, static)

6. **InteractionSystem.lua**
   - Detects proximity to interactive objects
   - Shows interaction prompts
   - Triggers actions (pick up items, open doors, etc.)

7. **AudioManager.lua**
   - Ambient sound playback (looped, fading)
   - Music transitions (crossfades, volume changes)
   - Sound effect triggers
   - Spatial audio (3D sound for door slams, footsteps, etc.)

8. **MovementController.lua**
   - Player movement (walking, running, jumping)
   - Speed variables (walk speed, sprint speed)
   - Footstep sounds (synced with movement)
   - Ground detection

### Key Technical Challenges

1. **Seamless Transitions**
   - Cutscenes must flow smoothly into player control
   - Audio crossfades should be smooth (use TweenService)
   - Camera animations should feel natural

2. **Phone Battery Drain**
   - Battery should decrease realistically over time
   - Battery display should update in real-time
   - Glitch effect when battery is low (foreshadowing Act 2)

3. **Ambient Soundscape**
   - Layer multiple ambient sounds (wind, distant sounds, silence)
   - Use spatial audio for 3D effects
   - Crossfade between different environments

4. **First-Person Immersion**
   - Keep player in first-person at all times
   - Even during cutscenes, camera should stay first-person
   - Subtle animations (head bob, hand visible in some contexts)

5. **Distance & Scale**
   - Walk time should feel realistic (~45 minutes in-game, compressed to ~8 minutes player time)
   - Factory should be large and imposing
   - Camera sensitivity should feel responsive
